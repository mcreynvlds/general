About DDN

Building better homes through craftsmanship, transparency & care

DDN Construction is a Barrie-based renovation and construction company delivering high-quality residential renovations throughout Barrie and Simcoe County. We specialize in kitchen renovations, bathroom remodels, basement finishing, and full-home transformations, combining skilled craftsmanship with a clear, organized process from start to finish.

Homeowners choose DDN for reliable communication, clean and respectful job sites, and work that’s built to last. From the initial consultation to the final walkthrough, we focus on creating functional, well-finished spaces that add long-term value and improve how you live in your home.
