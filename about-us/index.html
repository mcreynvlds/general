Our story

Barrie builders who sweat the details

DDN Construction is a Barrie-based residential construction and renovation company serving homeowners across Barrie and Simcoe County. We specialize in high-quality renovations and residential construction projects delivered with skilled craftsmanship, transparent pricing, and a dependable, well-managed process.

From kitchens and bathrooms to basements and full-home remodels, we approach every project with care, organization, and respect for your home. Our focus is on clear expectations, clean job sites, and quality construction that’s built to last — so homeowners can feel confident and informed at every stage of the build.
