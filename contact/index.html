FAQ

Quick answers about working with DDN

Don’t see your question? Give us a call at 249-989-1441.

What areas does DDN serve?

We’re based in Barrie and serve homeowners across Simcoe County and surrounding communities.

What type of projects do you take on?

We focus on residential renovations and construction, including kitchens, bathrooms, basements, full-home remodels, and interior upgrades.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes — after a quick conversation and site visit (when needed), we’ll provide a clear estimate based on your scope and goals.

How long do renovations usually take?

Timelines vary by project size and complexity, but we’ll outline a realistic schedule before work begins and keep you updated throughout.

Will I get a detailed quote?

Absolutely. Our quotes clearly define scope, materials allowances (if applicable), and what’s included so you know what to expect.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

If permits are required, we can guide you through the process and coordinate the necessary steps to keep the project compliant.

How do you handle changes during the project?

Any changes are discussed and approved before moving forward, with clear pricing and updated timelines when needed.

How do I get started with DDN Construction?

Fill out our contact form or call us with a few details about your project, and we’ll book a consultation to plan next steps.
